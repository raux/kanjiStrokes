01 BEGIN A DESCENT

The same seed always builds the same dungeon. Share it to compare descents.

How it goes. Each kanji’s strokes are its health. A well-placed stroke lands; a wild one misses and spills ink. Three misses and the kanji slips away — it will haunt a later floor, harder. Lose all resolve and the descent ends.

Floor 1 is bundled and works offline. Deeper floors fetch stroke data from KanjiVG.
